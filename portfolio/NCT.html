Redeveloping the North Country Trail

This project was my very first major project as a graduate student at the UMN. As a part of my GIS Project Management course with Susana McMaster, my classmates and I were tasked with interfacing with the North Country Trail Association team and synthesizing a new trail route from their specifications. My role was to find all of the lakes, rivers, wetlands, campsites, existing roads and trails, and land parcels. Then, I had to classify them based on a 1-7 scale, 1 being “Yes, let's build here!” and 7 being “This is impossible to walk on”. That data was then rasterized and combined into one big raster layer where each cell was attributed to a classification number. The result is what you see before you!
